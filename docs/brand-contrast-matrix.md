## Rules this table supports

- Body text on any light neutral: Black, Catholic Blue, Catholic Red, Bright Blue.
- Large text on White also tolerates Bright Red, Light Red and Lux; Light Red and Lux fail on Sandstone and Limestone, so treat them as White-only.
- Never text on a light neutral: Yellow, Light Blue-Green, Light Blue, Grey.
- Reversed fills for panels and buttons: White on Catholic Blue, White on Catholic Red.
- Focus indicator: Bright Blue clears 3:1 non-text contrast (SC 1.4.11) on White (6.83:1), Sandstone (6.17:1) and Limestone (5.76:1), and the SC 2.4.13 minimum area. On Grey it is only 2.90:1, so do not place a Bright Blue ring on a Grey fill; use Catholic Blue there instead.
- WCAG 2.2 SC 2.4.11: focus must not be hidden. The sticky header reserves `--header-height` and every focus ring sits inside the layer.
